Common questions

Is Tendra a replacement for therapy?

No. Tendra is designed for everyday stress and quick emotional check-ins — not crisis care or clinical treatment. If you're in crisis, please contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away.

Is my journal private?

Yes. Journal entries and mood check-ins are private to your account and are never shared or made public.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Tendra, and choose Cancel Subscription.

Something's broken — how do I report it?

Email us at the address above with a short description and, if possible, a screenshot. We read every message and usually reply within a couple of days.
